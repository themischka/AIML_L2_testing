Everyone picks for themselves, by Yuri Levitansky
Everyone picks for themselves,
A woman, a religion, a road.
To serve a devil or a prophet,
Everyone chooses for themselves.

Everyone picks for oneself,
Words of love and words of prayers.
Swords for fighting, swords for battles,
Everyone picks for oneself.

Everyone picks for oneself,
Armour and shields, sticks and patches.
The measure of final reckoning,
Everyone picks for oneself.

Everyone picks for themselves,
I, as well, as best I can,
To no one, I will put no blame on,
Everyone picks for themselves.
